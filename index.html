FAQ

Questions agents ask

What areas do you cover?

Polk County (Lakeland, Winter Haven, Davenport, Haines City and nearby), the Orlando area and the Tampa Bay area. For Orlando and Tampa Bay addresses, any travel fee is confirmed before you book. If you’re unsure about an address, email it and we’ll confirm.

How fast will I get my media?

Photos are delivered in 24–48 hours and reels in 48–72 hours after the shoot. Need the reel sooner? Add the 24-hour rush for $50.

How does founding pricing work?

The first 5 agents who book get the founding price on every package for their first 3 listings, plus a free “Just Sold” reel when one of those listings closes. In return we ask for permission to show the work in our portfolio. An honest review is appreciated but never required.

What’s in a listing reel?

A 30–60 second vertical video (9:16) with a hook, the key rooms and features, music, on-screen text and a call to action. It’s sized for Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook and YouTube Shorts. One revision round is included.

Can I use the photos and reel on the MLS and social media?

Yes. The media is delivered for marketing that listing on the MLS, your website and your social channels.

Do you shoot larger homes?

Yes. Photos are priced by size up to 5,000 sq ft. Larger homes get a quick custom quote. Email the address and square footage.

How do the monthly plans work?

You pick a plan for the number of listings you usually take each month. Every plan listing gets photos and a Listing Reel, plus extra reel cuts and priority scheduling. Plans have a 3-month minimum and then run month-to-month with 30 days’ notice. Unused listings roll over one month.

Is virtual twilight allowed on the MLS?

Virtual twilight images are digitally altered, so we label them that way and always deliver the original daytime photo too. Check your MLS rules for disclosure.
